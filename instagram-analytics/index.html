What is Rank Kiwi's Instagram Analysis?

Rank Kiwi is a lightweight browser extension that scans a creator's public Instagram library (25, 50, or 100 posts). Instead of showing you an endless reverse-chronological feed, it calculates the creator's true median view count and generates a relative Outlier Score (e.g. 5.4× typical views) for every Reel. You immediately see which topics, hooks, and formats broke through audience fatigue without manually recording a single spreadsheet cell.

Why analyze another creator's content?

Native Instagram feeds are optimized to keep you consuming entertainment, not conducting strategic research. When studying niche leaders or competitors, standard scrolling creates blind spots.

Isolate Proven Formats

Identify the exact narrative angles, on-screen text hooks, and editing rhythms that generated 3× to 10× more reach than the creator's standard uploads.

Eliminate Follower Bias

A 100,000-view Reel on an account with 2 million followers is normal baseline reach. The same 100,000 views on a creator with 15,000 followers is a massive viral anomaly. Rank Kiwi benchmarks relatively.

Build Swipe Files in Seconds

Export full video URLs, verified play counts, captions, and outlier multipliers directly to CSV or paste seamlessly into your team's Notion editorial database.

Transparent Platform Limitations

Rank Kiwi is an ethical, client-side research tool that reads publicly rendered DOM elements. It is not an unofficial API wrapper:

  • Hidden Metrics: If a creator toggles "Hide like and view counts" on an individual post, Instagram omits those numbers from public HTML. Rank Kiwi displays these as Hidden rather than guessing.
  • Private Profiles: Rank Kiwi cannot scan private, blocked, or age-restricted accounts.
  • Single Post Permalinks: You cannot calculate an Outlier Score on a standalone post permalink because establishing a median baseline requires a creator library sample.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about analyzing Instagram creator content with Rank Kiwi.

What Instagram content can Rank Kiwi analyze?

Rank Kiwi is optimized for creator profile pages, specifically the /reels/ tab. On Reels, Instagram publishes public view counts, making it possible to calculate non-skewed median baselines and Outlier Scores.

Do I need to log into Rank Kiwi with my Instagram password?

Never. Rank Kiwi does not require your password, personal account credentials, or cookies. It runs locally inside Google Chrome's secure client sandbox, reading only the content publicly visible on your active tab.

Can Rank Kiwi analyze private Instagram profiles?

No. Rank Kiwi respects platform privacy settings and cannot access private, invite-only, or age-gated profiles.

What is the difference between sorting by Views and Outlier Score?

Sorting by Views shows raw popularity, which often reflects account age and follower volume. Sorting by Outlier Score divides each post's views by the creator's median benchmark, surfacing the creative concepts that genuinely broke out regardless of channel size.

Can I export my Instagram research to a spreadsheet?

Yes. Rank Kiwi supports one-click exports to CSV, structured JSON payloads, and direct clipboard copying for immediate pasting into Google Sheets, Notion databases, or Microsoft Excel.

Does studying viral outliers guarantee my posts will go viral?

No. Rank Kiwi is a research and benchmarking tool, not an algorithmic prediction engine. It helps you identify which hooks, pacing structures, and formats resonated unusually well so you can ethically test similar editorial frameworks.
