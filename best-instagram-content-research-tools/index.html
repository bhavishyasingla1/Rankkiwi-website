Why Content Research is NOT Account Analytics

Account analytics tell you how your past posts performed. Content research tells you what formats work across the wider market so you can plan better future uploads. Effective content research requires:

1. Creator-Relative Baselines

A post with 500,000 views from an account with 10 million followers is actually an underperformer. A post with 200,000 views from an account with 8,000 followers is a massive 25× viral outlier. Without calculating creator-relative medians, raw view sorting leads you to copy tired ideas from huge accounts.

2. Frictionless On-Page Feed Sorting

Content research happens while browsing creator profiles. Having to copy profile links, paste them into remote dashboards, and wait for cloud scraping kills ideation flow. In-browser extension sorting allows instant inspection of top posts in context.

3. Fast Hook & Caption Export

Video editors and scriptwriters need to turn visual research into structured swipe files. Tools that export direct video links, view counts, like ratios, and outlier scores to CSV/JSON eliminate hours of manual copy-pasting.

Evaluation Criteria for Instagram Content Research

  • Outlier Discovery Engine: Ability to identify anomaly content using statistical median baselines.
  • Reels & Short-Form Support: Specialized parsing of Instagram Reels algorithms, play counts, and like ratios.
  • Sorting & Filtering: Flexible multidimensional sorting by views, comments, likes, and upload date.
  • Data Export: One-click export to CSV/JSON for swipe files and creative breakdowns.
  • Workflow Speed: Lightweight in-browser execution versus heavy cloud dashboard setups.
  • Price & Limits: Unrestricted feed scanning versus arbitrary post-count paywalls.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does an Outlier Score help Instagram content research? +

An Outlier Score calculates a post's view count relative to that creator's median benchmark over the sampled window. Instead of copying posts that only got views because the creator has 5 million followers, Outlier Scores highlight posts that achieved 3×, 5×, or 10× typical engagement. This reveals genuine format and hook innovation.

Is Rank Kiwi really 100% free with no post limits? +

Yes. Rank Kiwi operates entirely client-side within your browser's Chrome sandbox. Because there are no expensive remote servers processing video audio or storing scraped databases, Rank Kiwi is free forever ($0.00 USD) with no paywalls or credit cards.

Can I export research data from Instagram to Google Sheets? +

Yes. With Rank Kiwi, clicking the Export button immediately downloads a clean CSV or JSON file containing video URLs, captions, hooks, view counts, like ratios, and Outlier Scores ready to import directly into Google Sheets or Notion swipe files.

Does Rank Kiwi work on private Instagram profiles? +

No. Rank Kiwi exclusively researches public creator and business profiles that are publicly accessible in the browser, adhering strictly to public web standards and client privacy.
