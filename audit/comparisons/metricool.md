## Verified Facts & Current Offerings
- **Platforms Supported:** Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Twitch, Google Business.
- **Category:** All-in-one social media management, publishing, and analytics platform.
- **Workflow:** Web and mobile dashboard where users connect social accounts to schedule posts, manage inboxes, and view metrics.
- **Outlier Score:** Not available. Displays standard raw view counts and engagement metrics.
- **Publishing & Scheduling:** Visual content calendar with auto-publishing, best times to post, and multi-network cross-posting.
- **Free Tier:** Permanent free plan with 1 brand, 20 scheduled posts/month, and tracking for up to 5 competitor profiles.
- **Paid Plans:** Starter at $18/mo billed annually ($22/mo monthly), Advanced at $45/mo billed annually ($54/mo monthly), Custom enterprise tiers.

## Where Metricool is Stronger
1. Full social publishing suite: content scheduling, calendar visualization, and automatic publishing across multiple networks.
2. Unified social inbox for responding to comments and messages in one dashboard.
3. Multi-platform ad management and tracking for Meta and Google Ads.

## Where Rank Kiwi is Stronger
1. Pure focus on creator-relative content research without a 5-competitor account tracking cap.
2. Calculates median-based Outlier Scores on specific posts and videos.
3. Zero account creation, zero login requirements, and instant on-page activation in Chrome.

## Neutral Recommendation
- Choose Metricool if you need a reliable, affordable social media scheduler, calendar, and publishing manager for your owned profiles.
- Choose Rank Kiwi if your priority is reverse-engineering high-performing content and discovering viral outliers across dozens of creators in your niche.
