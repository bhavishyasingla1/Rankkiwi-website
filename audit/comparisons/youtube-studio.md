# Competitor Research Log: YouTube Studio

- **Tool Name:** YouTube Studio (Analytics)
- **Official Website:** [https://studio.youtube.com](https://studio.youtube.com)
- **Official Documentation Checked:** [https://support.google.com/youtube/topic/9257498](https://support.google.com/youtube/topic/9257498)
- **Date Checked:** October 5, 2026
- **Researcher:** Bhavishya Singla

## Verified Facts & Current Offerings
- **Platform Supported:** YouTube (Owned channels only).
- **Category:** Native First-Party Channel & Video Analytics.
- **Workflow:** First-party Google web dashboard and mobile app for authenticated channel owners and managers.
- **Access & Cost:** 100% free native feature provided by Google/YouTube for channel management.
- **Metrics Covered:** Real-time views, Impressions, Click-Through Rate (CTR), Average View Duration (AVD), Audience Retention curves, Traffic Sources, Estimated Revenue & RPM/CPM, Subscriber demographics.
- **Outlier Score:** No post-level relative Outlier Score multiplier across historical catalogs. Offers basic rank by views (e.g. "Latest video performance: 1 of 10") for recent uploads.
- **Key Purpose:** Analyzing and managing your own channel's private performance, viewer drop-off points, and monetization.

## Where YouTube Studio is Stronger
1. Direct access to private, ground-truth analytics that no third-party tool can legally access (revenue, RPM, audience retention graphs, real-time views).
2. Direct content and channel management: uploading thumbnails, editing metadata, moderating comments, and managing copyright.
3. Completely native, official, and free with zero third-party data latency.

## Where Rank Kiwi is Stronger
1. Works on *any* creator's public channel or video catalog—not just channels you own.
2. Calculates creator-relative Outlier Scores across visible video libraries.
3. Cross-platform support for Instagram Reels and posts alongside YouTube.
4. One-click spreadsheet exports (CSV/JSON) for competitor benchmarking.

## Neutral Recommendation
- Use YouTube Studio to deeply analyze your *own* channel's viewer retention, revenue, and audience demographics.
- Use Rank Kiwi when your research question is about *another* creator's public content, competitor benchmarking, or Instagram research.
