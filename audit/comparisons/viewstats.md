## Verified Facts & Current Offerings
- **Platforms Supported:** YouTube exclusively (Long-form videos and Shorts).
- **Category:** Dedicated YouTube creator intelligence and channel analytics platform.
- **Workflow:** Web intelligence platform with searchable database plus companion browser extension.
- **Outlier Score:** Supported. Features dedicated outlier detection algorithms indexing YouTube video performance against channel history.
- **Unique Tools:** Visual thumbnail search, title/thumbnail A/B testing detection, channel milestone projections, custom tracking collections, and proactive alerts.
- **Free Tier:** Free access to channel search, recent video views, basic outlier indicators, and subscriber milestones.
- **Paid Plans:** Pro tier starting at $49/mo (with promotional/introductory tiers around $20/mo), and Business plans starting at $249/mo with full historical search and API access.

## Where Viewstats is Stronger
1. Massive YouTube-specific index with years of historical channel data.
2. Advanced visual thumbnail search, comparison tools, and A/B test tracking.
3. Proactive channel alerts and custom tracking collections for studios.

## Where Rank Kiwi is Stronger
1. Cross-platform coverage: Evaluates Instagram Reels, Carousels, and Posts in addition to YouTube Shorts and long-form videos.
2. 100% free with no tiered paywalls on outlier calculations.
3. Lightweight client-side browser workflow directly integrated into the active social feed.

## Neutral Recommendation
- Choose Viewstats if you are a dedicated YouTube creator or studio needing comprehensive YouTube thumbnail search, alerts, and historical data.
- Choose Rank Kiwi if you produce content for both Instagram and YouTube and want a fast, free, in-browser tool to find outlier posts and videos.
