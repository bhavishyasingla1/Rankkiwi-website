## Verified Facts & Current Offerings
- **Platforms Supported:** Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube.
- **Workflow:** Browser extension operating locally on supported social media platforms.
- **Outlier Score:** Supported. Calculates post performance relative to a creator's median benchmark over recent posts.
- **Sorting & Filtering:** Supported. Sorts by views, likes, comments, engagement rate, upload date.
- **Exports & Downloads:** Exports data to CSV and JSON; supports direct video and image downloads.
- **Transcription:** Supported on Pro plan (500 minutes of AI audio transcription per month). Note: audio is processed via cloud transcription service.
- **Free Plan Limits:** Latest 25 posts or 1 week of visible content. Includes all sort modes, outlier scores, and exports within this limit.
- **Pro Plan Pricing:** $10/month billed annually ($120/year) or $15/month billed monthly. Unlocks up to 1,000 posts, custom post counts/date ranges, and transcription.

## Where Sort Feed is Stronger
1. Multi-platform coverage: Supports TikTok and Facebook in addition to Instagram and YouTube.
2. Historical batch scanning: Up to 1,000 posts on Pro plan.
3. Audio transcription: Integrated transcription for video dialogues.

## Where Rank Kiwi is Stronger
1. Completely free with no 25-post paywall on visible feed analysis.
2. Dedicated specialization on Instagram and YouTube with optimized local DOM parsing.
3. 100% client-side privacy: zero audio sent to third-party transcription servers.

## Neutral Recommendation
- Choose Sort Feed if you regularly research creators on TikTok or Facebook, or if you need automated speech-to-text audio transcription.
- Choose Rank Kiwi if you focus on Instagram and YouTube and want fast, free, unrestricted outlier research without monthly fees.
