## Verified Facts & Current Offerings
- **Platforms Supported:** Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, X (Twitter).
- **Category:** Cloud-based social media analytics, competitive benchmarking, and reporting suite.
- **Workflow:** Centralized web dashboard. Users connect owned brand profiles and manually add competitor accounts to track over time.
- **Outlier Score:** No post-level relative Outlier Score multiplier. Tracks overall engagement rates, average views, and industry benchmarks.
- **Exports & Reporting:** Automated white-label PDF reports, Excel, and CSV data exports.
- **Free Tier:** 14-day free trial only. No permanent free tier.
- **Paid Plan Pricing:**
  - Adapt: $82/month billed annually ($99/mo monthly).
  - Optimize: $124/month billed annually ($149/mo monthly).
  - Predict: $199/month billed annually ($249/mo monthly).

## Where Socialinsider is Stronger
1. Cross-network brand tracking across 6 major social platforms.
2. Automated agency reporting with white-label client PDF generation.
3. Multi-year historical analytics storage and industry-level benchmark datasets.

## Where Rank Kiwi is Stronger
1. 100% free with zero monthly fees ($0 vs $82–$199/mo).
2. Instant in-browser creator research without creating accounts, configuring dashboards, or waiting for data ingestion.
3. Post-level creator-relative Outlier Scores (e.g., 4.2× median) to evaluate individual creative hooks.

## Neutral Recommendation
- Choose Socialinsider if you are an agency or enterprise brand that needs automated client PDF reports and multi-year brand benchmarking.
- Choose Rank Kiwi if you are a creator or content researcher wanting fast, free, on-page outlier discovery while browsing creator feeds.
