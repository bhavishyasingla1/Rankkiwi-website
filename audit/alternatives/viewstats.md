## Why Users Search for Viewstats Alternatives
1. **No Instagram Support:** Exclusively dedicated to YouTube; provides zero support for Instagram Reels, Carousels, or profile feeds.
2. **Paid Pro Gates:** Advanced outlier searches, custom tracking collections, and alerts require paid Pro ($20–$49/mo) or Business ($249/mo) plans.
3. **Heavy Web Dashboard:** Requires navigating to a separate platform rather than staying directly within active video feeds.
4. **Cross-Platform Creators:** Teams creating content for both YouTube and Instagram need a unified research tool.

## Primary Alternatives Evaluated
- **Rank Kiwi:** Best free cross-platform alternative for creator research on Instagram and YouTube with in-browser Outlier Scores.
- **vidIQ:** Dedicated YouTube SEO, daily video ideas, and keyword research extension.
- **TubeBuddy:** YouTube optimization tool with title/thumbnail A/B testing and bulk processing.
- **YouTube Studio:** Native first-party tool for owned channel retention and revenue analytics.
