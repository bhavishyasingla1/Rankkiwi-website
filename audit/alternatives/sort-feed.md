## Why Users Search for Sort Feed Alternatives
1. **Free Tier Limitations:** Sort Feed free plan limits analysis to latest 25 posts or 1 week of visible content. Users researching complete creator archives hit a paywall.
2. **Platform Specialization:** Users exclusively researching Instagram and YouTube do not need paid TikTok and Facebook features.
3. **Data Privacy:** Sort Feed Pro audio transcription uploads video audio to external cloud servers; privacy-conscious users prefer 100% local client-side processing.
4. **Subscription Overhead:** Sort Feed Pro costs $10/mo billed annually ($120/yr) or $15/mo monthly.

## Primary Alternatives Evaluated
- **Rank Kiwi:** 100% Free browser extension for Instagram & YouTube. No 25-post paywall on visible feeds. In-browser Outlier Scores, feed sorting, CSV/JSON exports.
- **Viewstats:** Deep YouTube intelligence platform with historical video index and visual thumbnail packaging search.
- **Socialinsider:** Multi-channel social analytics platform for agency client reporting and cross-platform benchmarking.
