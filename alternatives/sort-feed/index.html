Looking for a Sort Feed Alternative?

Sort Feed is a popular browser extension for sorting social feeds across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and YouTube. But depending on your platform mix, research depth, privacy priorities, and budget, other tools may be a better fit.

By Bhavishya Singla • Last reviewed: October 5, 2026 • Sourced from official docs & pricing
Editorial Transparency: We build Rank Kiwi, one of the primary alternatives featured here. Our objective on this page is to present multiple legitimate market alternatives, acknowledge Sort Feed's strengths honestly, and explain who should choose which tool.

Why Look for a Sort Feed Alternative?

Sort Feed is effective for creators who need quick sorting across multiple networks. However, content creators, editors, and strategists frequently search for alternatives due to specific workflow needs:

1. Free Tier Scan Limits

Sort Feed's free tier restricts research to the latest 25 posts or 1 week of visible content. Users wanting to audit full creator back-catalogs without paying $10–$15/month seek tools with no post-count paywalls.

2. Instagram & YouTube Specialization

Many creators only publish to Instagram and YouTube. They prefer dedicated DOM extractors built specifically for Reels and YouTube Shorts without paying for unused TikTok or Facebook features.

3. Client-Side Privacy

Sort Feed's cloud transcription service uploads video audio to external servers. Privacy-focused creators who only need statistical outlier detection prefer extensions that execute 100% locally in the browser sandbox.

4. Deeper YouTube Packaging Insights

Creators heavily focused on YouTube long-form often require visual thumbnail search engines and channel outlier alerts, which are outside Sort Feed's core feature set.

What Sort Feed Does Well

To be completely fair, Sort Feed is a capable tool that serves specific workflows better than its alternatives:

  • Broad Platform Support: It supports TikTok and Facebook in addition to Instagram and YouTube, which neither Rank Kiwi nor Viewstats covers.
  • Audio Transcription: Its Pro plan provides 500 minutes per month of speech-to-text transcription, helping scriptwriters extract dialogue from viral videos.
  • Deep Batch Scanning: On Pro, users can scan up to 1,000 historical posts across supported platforms with custom date range filters.
  • Built-in Media Downloader: Direct downloading for images and videos from supported feeds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free alternative to Sort Feed?
For Instagram and YouTube creator research, Rank Kiwi is the best free alternative. It provides creator-relative Outlier Scores, sorting, filtering, and data exports with no 25-post paywall on visible feeds.
Why does Sort Feed limit free scans to 25 posts?
Sort Feed operates on a freemium SaaS model. It offers the latest 25 posts or 7 days of content for free, requiring users to upgrade to its Pro subscription ($10/month billed annually or $15/month monthly) to unlock deeper scans up to 1,000 posts.
Which alternative is best for TikTok and Facebook?
If you specifically need to sort creator feeds on TikTok or Facebook, Sort Feed itself is often the best fit. Most alternatives (including Rank Kiwi and Viewstats) specialize in Instagram and YouTube.
Can Rank Kiwi transcribe video audio like Sort Feed Pro?
No. Rank Kiwi is focused purely on content metrics, Outlier Scores, sorting, and data exports. It does not provide speech-to-text audio transcription.
How does Rank Kiwi's Outlier Score compare to Sort Feed's?
Both tools use creator-relative median benchmarking to avoid skew from one-off viral hits. Rank Kiwi calculates the median across loaded feed items dynamically in real time, making it free and immediately accessible without an account.
