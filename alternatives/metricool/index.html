Looking for a Metricool Alternative?

Metricool is one of the most popular all-in-one social publishing and management suites. But if your primary challenge is creator content research, reverse-engineering viral hooks, or finding a simpler scheduler, specialized alternatives exist.

By Bhavishya Singla • Last reviewed: October 5, 2026 • Sourced from official docs & pricing
Editorial Transparency: We build Rank Kiwi, a dedicated creator research extension. Metricool is primarily a publishing and scheduling suite, whereas Rank Kiwi is a dedicated content research tool. We do not pretend they are interchangeable.

Why Look for a Metricool Alternative?

Metricool is well-regarded for scheduling and basic analytics. However, users search for alternatives for several common reasons:

1. Competitor Tracking Limits

Metricool's free tier restricts competitor tracking to 5 profiles total. Content researchers wanting to survey 30–50 niche competitors hit an immediate barrier.

2. Lack of Outlier Multipliers

Metricool sorts content by absolute metrics (views, interactions). It does not compute creator-relative Outlier Scores to identify viral hooks that outpaced a creator's typical median baseline.

3. Live On-Page Browsing vs Dashboard

Metricool requires manually typing competitor handles into a dashboard. Creators prefer lightweight extensions that extract metrics directly as they browse Instagram and YouTube.

4. Need Research, Not Publishing

Video editors, copywriters, and research analysts do not need visual calendars, post schedulers, or social inboxes—they just want fast competitor content data.

What Metricool Does Well

Metricool is one of the most comprehensive budget-friendly social suites on the market:

  • All-in-One Content Calendar: Visual scheduling and auto-publishing across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn, and Pinterest.
  • Best Time to Post: Data-driven heatmaps showing when your specific followers are most active.
  • Unified Inbox: Reply to comments and direct messages across networks from a single screen.
  • Ad Spend Tracking: Integrated reporting for Google Ads, Meta Ads, and TikTok Ads.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Rank Kiwi schedule posts like Metricool?
No. Rank Kiwi is exclusively a creator research and analytics extension. It does not connect to auto-publishing APIs. If you need post scheduling, Metricool or Buffer is built for that workflow.
Why do creators use Rank Kiwi alongside Metricool?
Many creators use Metricool to manage their publishing calendar and queue, but use Rank Kiwi during pre-production to research competitors, find viral video hooks, and calculate Outlier Scores on Instagram and YouTube.
What are Metricool's free tier limits?
As of October 5, 2026, Metricool's free plan allows 1 brand, 20 scheduled posts per month, and tracking up to 5 competitor profiles. Paid plans remove scheduling limits and expand competitor quotas.
Does Rank Kiwi limit how many competitors I can analyze?
No. Because Rank Kiwi runs directly in your browser on public web pages, you can inspect hundreds of creator profiles on Instagram and YouTube with zero limits.
