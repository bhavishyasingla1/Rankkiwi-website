What is Rank Kiwi's YouTube Analysis?

Rank Kiwi is an in-browser creator research workspace that evaluates a YouTube channel's recent uploads against its own median performance baseline. Instead of judging videos by raw subscriber counts, it calculates an Outlier Score (e.g. 4.2× channel median). This immediately reveals which packaging concepts, titles, and thumbnails generated breakthrough reach, and allows you to download uncompressed 1080p thumbnail images and export clean CSV datasets in seconds.

Why raw subscribers and views mislead

When researching YouTube competitors, looking only at raw view counts produces severe false positives and false negatives.

Channel A: Large Authority

1,500,000 Subscribers • Typical video: 120,000 views. A video with 150,000 views here is merely typical baseline distribution (1.2×). It does not represent an exceptional packaging breakthrough.

Channel B: Emergent Creator

25,000 Subscribers • Typical video: 8,000 views. A video with 85,000 views here is an explosive 10.6× Outlier. That title and thumbnail packaging reached 10× beyond their subscriber core.

Recent vs Historical Outliers

YouTube's native "Most Popular" tab surfaces videos uploaded years ago. Rank Kiwi analyzes a recent sample (e.g. last 50 uploads) to show what packaging works in today's algorithmic environment.

Dedicated Analysis for Long-Form Videos & YouTube Shorts

Long-form and vertical formats have completely different distribution curves. Rank Kiwi treats them as distinct datasets.

Long-Form Videos

youtube.com/@[channel]/videos

Scan the creator's dedicated long-form catalog. Evaluate titles, video duration, published dates, and view counts against channel median.

  • Isolate thumbnail title combinations that drove 3×+ outliers
  • Filter by minimum view thresholds (e.g. > 50,000 views)
  • Download full 1920×1080 HD thumbnail images
YouTube Shorts

youtube.com/@[channel]/shorts

Shorts have high viral velocity but volatile baselines. Rank Kiwi scans the vertical catalog and benchmarks Shorts strictly against other Shorts.

  • Zero distortion from long-form video metrics
  • Rank by view multipliers (e.g. 5× or 12× baseline)
  • Export hook titles and URLs directly to Google Sheets

Transparent YouTube Limitations

Rank Kiwi parses data publicly accessible from YouTube's desktop web interface:

  • Live Streams: The /streams tab uses different DOM metadata schemas; Rank Kiwi is designed specifically for /videos and /shorts.
  • Watch Pages: Analysis must be triggered on channel catalog tabs, not while watching a single video on /watch?v=....
  • Shorts Comments: YouTube's channel Shorts grid exposes views and likes in DOM hover states, but comments require opening individual Shorts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about researching YouTube channels with Rank Kiwi.

How does Rank Kiwi calculate a YouTube channel's median?

Rank Kiwi extracts the view counts of the sampled videos (e.g. 50 videos), sorts them numerically, and selects the exact middle value. This median represents typical performance without being distorted by one viral runaway hit.

Can I analyze YouTube Shorts separately from long-form videos?

Yes. When you open the channel's /shorts tab, Rank Kiwi computes a Shorts-specific median. Your Shorts metrics are never combined with or distorted by long-form video views.

Can I download full-resolution YouTube thumbnails?

Yes. Rank Kiwi includes a direct thumbnail fetcher that retrieves the maximum resolution upload (1080p maxresdefault.jpg) directly from YouTube's media servers.

Why is Outlier Score better than sorting by "Most Popular" on YouTube?

YouTube's native "Most Popular" tab surfaces videos from 6 years ago that accumulated views over half a decade. Rank Kiwi analyzes recent sample batches (e.g. the last 50 uploads) to show what packaging works in today's algorithm.

Do I need YouTube API keys or an account?

No API keys or YouTube account permissions are required. Rank Kiwi reads only the publicly visible DOM elements in your active tab locally in Chrome.
