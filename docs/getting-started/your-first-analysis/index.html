Your First Creator Analysis

A complete beginner walkthrough from navigating to a creator profile to extracting winning hooks and Outlier Scores.

Step 1: Open Creator Profile

Open Google Chrome and navigate to any public creator profile on Instagram or channel on YouTube.

For example, navigate to an Instagram creator like https://instagram.com/creator_handle/reels/ or a YouTube channel like https://youtube.com/@channel_name.

Requirement: The profile must be public. Private Instagram accounts hide post feeds from non-approved visitors, preventing client-side data inspection.

Step 2: Switch Content Surface

Select the dedicated video feed tab for accurate metric collection.

  • On Instagram: Switch to the /reels/ tab (e.g. instagram.com/creator/reels/). Rank Kiwi focuses exclusively on video Reels because they display verified public view counts. Analysis works directly from the creator's Reels tab.
  • On YouTube: Switch to the Videos tab for long-form uploads or the Shorts tab for vertical short-form catalogs. Rank Kiwi prompts you to switch to the Videos or Shorts tab if you are on the channel home page.

Step 3: Open Rank Kiwi

Click the pinned kiwi icon in your Chrome toolbar or the on-page analysis button.

Rank Kiwi verifies that you are on an active, supported creator URL and opens the analysis configuration panel.

Step 4: Choose Sample Depth

Choose how many recent videos or Reels Rank Kiwi should inspect to compute the creator's median benchmark.

Sample Option Recommended Use Case Average Scan Duration
25 Posts Fast spot checks; recently published hook trends ~2 to 4 seconds
50 Posts Recommended default; balanced and stable median ~5 to 8 seconds
100 Posts Comprehensive deep catalog study across seasons ~10 to 15 seconds

Step 5: Run Benchmark Calculation

Click the green Analyze button to begin local collection.

Rank Kiwi scans the visible videos in your browser, extracts public engagement metrics (views, likes, comments, publication dates), and computes the non-skewed Median Baseline for that creator.

During collection, you will see a live progress counter. If you need to stop early, you can safely cancel the scan.

Step 6: Sort Outlier Score

Once analysis finishes, Rank Kiwi displays the results grid with Outlier Scores computed for every post.

By default, posts are ordered by Outlier Score (High to Low):

  • 1.0× — Typical Performance: Post performed at the creator's exact median.
  • 2.0× to 3.5× — Strong Performer: Earned double to triple typical creator views.
  • 4.0× to 10.0×+ — Breakout Outlier: A statistical anomaly that massively outperformed the baseline.

Step 7: Inspect Content Details

Click on any outlier card in the grid to open the Content Details inspector.

The detail view reveals:

  • In-Modal HTML5 Video Player: For Instagram Reels, enjoy full 9:16 video playback with seekbar, volume controls, and duration display.
  • One-Click MP4 Download: Download the verified MP4 video file directly to your computer with sanitized naming.
  • YouTube HD Thumbnails: For YouTube content, download crisp production thumbnails (up to 1080p).
  • Full Caption & Hook Text: Study opening hooks, copywriting structure, and calls-to-action without leaving the overlay.
  • Metrics Breakdown: Exact view counts, like counts, comment counts, and Outlier Score multiplier.

Step 8: Export Your Research

Export your findings directly to your preferred research tool.

Click the Export dropdown in the top bar to select:

  • Native Excel (.xlsx): Generates genuine Open Packaging Conventions XML workbooks in-browser with formatted numeric columns.
  • CSV Download: Standard comma-separated spreadsheet file compatible with Google Sheets, Airtable, and Notion.
  • Copy to Clipboard: Dual-format copy that writes both tab-separated values (TSV) and styled HTML tables for instant pasting.
  • JSON Download: Indented JSON payload schema for data analysts and automated research workflows.
  • Batch Reel Downloads: Select multiple Reels and download clean MP4 files sequentially with a single click.

Congratulations! You have completed your first Rank Kiwi creator research analysis. You now have a proven workflow to isolate breakout content without manual spreadsheet scraping.
