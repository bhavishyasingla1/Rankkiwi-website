Getting Started with Rank Kiwi

Install the extension, understand the core mental model, and tour the workspace interface.

What Rank Kiwi Does

Rank Kiwi is a browser extension for creator-content research on Instagram and YouTube.

Rather than browsing posts chronologically or guessing which content broke out, Rank Kiwi calculates creator-relative performance benchmarks and identifies posts that performed dramatically above that creator's typical baseline.

The Core Mental Model: Choose a creator → Analyze visible content → Rank the content → Identify unusually strong performers → Study the outliers → Use findings to make informed content decisions.

Rank Kiwi is not a generic analytics dashboard. It requires no social account logins, accesses zero passwords, and stores zero analyzed creator data on external servers. All operations execute 100% locally inside your browser sandbox.

Installation & Browser Setup

Installing Rank Kiwi takes under 30 seconds from the Chrome Web Store.

Step 1: Install from the Chrome Web Store

Visit the official Rank Kiwi extension listing on the Chrome Web Store and click Add to Chrome.

Step 2: Pin the Extension for Quick Access

After Chrome confirms installation, click the puzzle piece icon (Extensions) in your browser toolbar and click the Pin icon next to Rank Kiwi. This keeps the Rank Kiwi kiwi icon visible whenever you browse Instagram or YouTube.

Step 3: Confirm Browser Permissions

Rank Kiwi operates directly on the active webpage to read visible post information. Chrome grants access to active tabs on supported creator sites without requiring broad browser-wide tracking permissions.

Supported Platforms & Surfaces

Rank Kiwi detects creator profiles when you navigate to specific platform surfaces:

Platform Supported Surface Optimal URL Structure Content Types Analyzed
Instagram Reels Tab (Recommended) instagram.com/creator/reels/ Video Reels exclusively with verified view/play metrics
Instagram Main Profile Grid instagram.com/creator/ Video Reels only (static photos and carousels are filtered out to keep video baselines accurate)
YouTube Channel Videos Tab youtube.com/@channel/videos Long-form video uploads with anti-leakage channel validation
YouTube Channel Shorts Tab youtube.com/@channel/shorts YouTube Shorts video feed with live view enrichment

Note on Unsupported Surfaces: Rank Kiwi is designed for creator catalogs and channel profiles. It does not analyze your personal home feed, algorithm explore pages, or private direct message threads.

Analysis Sample Controls

Rank Kiwi allows you to choose your analysis depth before scanning:

  • 25 Posts: Ideal for rapid spot checks and recently published content trends.
  • 50 Posts: Recommended standard sample size for accurate median baseline calculation.
  • 100 Posts: Deep catalog research for established creators across several months.
  • Custom: Enter a custom item count based on your research scope.
