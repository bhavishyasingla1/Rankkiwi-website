Platform Guide

Instagram Creator Research Manual

Comprehensive guide to analyzing Instagram creator profiles, scanning Reels, understanding public metrics, and navigating platform boundaries.

How Rank Kiwi Analyzes Instagram.

Rank Kiwi delivers creator performance intelligence across public Instagram creator and business profiles through a dedicated, Reels-only video pipeline.

When you visit an Instagram profile or Reels tab, Rank Kiwi identifies the creator handle, inspects in-memory client-side state, extracts public video metrics, and calculates the creator's median view benchmark to surface breakout viral Reels.

Zero Account Requirements: Rank Kiwi does not ask for your Instagram password, does not connect via private user tokens, and does not perform automated actions on your personal account. All operations run 100% locally in your browser sandbox on public page data.

The Reels-Only Video Architecture

Why Rank Kiwi evaluates video Reels exclusively, discarding non-video static posts and carousels.

The Need for Benchmark Purity

Instagram's web architecture handles video and photo metrics fundamentally differently:

  • Video Reels Expose Play Counts: Every public Reel displays true video play/view counts. This allows calculating exact view-to-median Outlier Scores.
  • Static Photos & Carousels Omit Plays: Instagram does not publicly expose view or play counts on static images or multi-slide carousels—only likes and comments.

Including static images alongside Reels would inject uncounted zero-view items, severely distorting the creator's median benchmark. To guarantee actionable creator intelligence, Rank Kiwi focuses exclusively on video Reels for an authentic, apples-to-apples comparison.

Deep Relay / XDT Store Extraction

Rank Kiwi queries Instagram's internal client-side React/Relay store cache (getRelayStoreRecords, clipsRec, mediaRec) to retrieve accurate play counts (play_count, video_play_count, video_view_count) directly within your active session. If internal store data is incomplete, it seamlessly falls back to DOM card scrapers and background fiber probes.

In-Modal 9:16 Video Player

Inspect winning Reels with a responsive vertical video player built directly into the analysis workbench.

Clicking any Reel card in the results grid opens the Content Details modal featuring our custom 9:16 player:

  • Custom Playback Controls: Seamless play, pause, timeline seeking bar, and countdown duration timer.
  • Dynamic Soundwave Animation: Animated wave bars pulse dynamically to indicate active playback and synchronize with pause/seek events.
  • Audio Sync & Mute: Inline volume controls with immediate mute/unmute toggling.
  • Clean State Isolation: Selecting different Reels cleanly reinitializes the player with the active shortcode, revoking prior object URLs to prevent browser memory leaks.

Reel Video Downloads (MP4)

Directly save public creator Reel video files for offline swipe files and pacing deconstruction.

Rank Kiwi supports both single-item and batch MP4 acquisition:

  • Single Reel Download: Click Download Reel (MP4) inside the Content Details modal or use the inline download button on any Reel card to save the active video stream.
  • Toolbar Batch Downloads: Use the multi-select checkboxes on cards in the workbench grid to select multiple Reels, then click Download Selected in the top toolbar.
  • Standardized Safe Naming: Files are saved automatically to your default Chrome downloads folder as rankkiwi_[creator]_[reelId].mp4, with path traversal sequences safely sanitized.

Download Reels from Explore

Save inspiring Reels directly from Instagram Explore before initiating any full creator profile analysis.

While browsing Instagram's Explore surface (instagram.com/explore/), Rank Kiwi places a quick-action download overlay directly onto Reel cards. You don't need to run a creator scan or wait for metrics calculation—simply hover over any Reel on Explore and click the download button to grab the video MP4 immediately.

For in-depth step-by-step guidance on all explore downloading workflows, read our dedicated Explore Downloads Guide.

Instagram Custom Filters

Filter noisy feeds to isolate specific viral performance thresholds and recent date windows.

Rank Kiwi provides dedicated filters for Instagram creator catalogs:

  • Outlier Multiplier Threshold: Show only items with an Outlier Score of 2.0× (double median), 3.0× (top tier), or 5.0×+ (breakout anomaly).
  • Date Windows: Restrict results to Reels published within the last 30, 60, or 90 days to avoid comparing historical spikes with recent baseline shifts.
  • Keyword Search: Instant text search across caption hooks, hashtags, and keywords.

Instagram Platform Boundaries & Limitations

Rank Kiwi respects platform constraints and works strictly with publicly accessible data.

Private Profiles

Rank Kiwi cannot scan private Instagram accounts. If a creator account requires follow approval, the post DOM is protected by Instagram's authentication wall and cannot be collected.

Hidden View or Like Counts

If a creator has explicitly enabled Instagram's privacy setting to "Hide like and view counts" on a specific post, Instagram omits that number from the public DOM. Rank Kiwi normalizes missing metrics to null and displays — rather than fabricating estimates, placing uncounted items safely at the bottom of sorted lists.

Single-Page Navigation (SPA)

Instagram uses client-side history transitions without full page reloads. If Rank Kiwi ever fails to refresh creator context when clicking between profiles, simply reload the browser tab (Cmd + R / F5).
