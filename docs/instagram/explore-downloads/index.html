Feature Guide • v1.0.1

Instagram Video Downloads Guide.

Save original MP4 videos directly from Instagram Explore and creator profiles without watermarks.

Instant Video Downloads Overview.

Rank Kiwi version 1.0.1 introduces direct video downloading on Instagram. When analyzing competitor feeds or researching trending video formats on Instagram's Explore page, you can save reference MP4 files directly to your machine with a single click.

Unlike generic web downloaders filled with intrusive pop-up ads and re-encoding watermarks, Rank Kiwi accesses the pristine MP4 stream already buffered by your browser, preserving original frame rate, vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, and audio quality.

Supported Locations: Works on both the Instagram Explore feed (instagram.com/explore/) and any creator profile's Reels tab (instagram.com/[username]/reels/).

How to Download Videos.

  1. Navigate to Explore or a Creator's Reels Grid: Open Instagram in Google Chrome with the Rank Kiwi extension enabled. Go to either instagram.com/explore/ or any public creator profile and click their Reels tab.
  2. Spot the Download Action Button: Hover over any visible video thumbnail or open the video in modal view. You will see the Rank Kiwi overlay button with a download icon and tooltip prompt.
  3. Click Download: Rank Kiwi resolves the original CDN media URL and invokes Chrome's native chrome.downloads API.
  4. Locate File on Your Disk: The video saves directly to your default browser downloads directory. The filename automatically includes the creator handle and shortcode for effortless organization:
    [creator]_[shortcode]_outlier.mp4

Why Creators Download References.

Frame-by-Frame Hook Analysis

Import the MP4 into Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut, or CapCut to inspect exact cut pacing, text graphic keyframes, and sound effect timing.

Production Moodboarding

Build offline reference folders for video production crews and client pitch presentations without broken Instagram web links.

Audio Transcription & Modeling

Extract spoken hooks using Whisper or AI transcribers to study linguistic patterns in breakout videos.

Zero-Proxy Client-Side Privacy Guarantee

Security and data privacy are paramount in Rank Kiwi's design:

  • No Remote Proxy Servers: Many third-party download extensions route your web requests through third-party servers to scrape videos. Rank Kiwi never routes video data through external servers.
  • Local Chrome Sandbox: The download originates strictly between your Chrome browser and Meta's public content delivery network (CDN).
  • Zero Credential Access: Rank Kiwi never touches your Instagram session cookies, auth tokens, or passwords.

Troubleshooting Common Download Issues

1. Chrome shows "Multiple files download blocked" prompt

If you download several reference videos in rapid succession, Chrome may ask for permission to download multiple files from instagram.com. Click Allow in the browser prompt to continue.

2. Download button does not appear on static photos

Rank Kiwi v1.0.1 is strictly optimized for video content. The download action only appears on video Reels, not static photo posts or single-image carousels.

3. Private profile videos cannot be downloaded

Rank Kiwi operates exclusively on public Instagram creator profiles. Content behind private follower walls is intentionally inaccessible.
