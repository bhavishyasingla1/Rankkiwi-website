Sorting & Custom Filters

A reference guide to ranking creator content and using multi-attribute filters to isolate winning content hooks.

Supported Sorting Orders

Rank Kiwi supports multiple sorting dimensions to answer different research questions:

Sort Option What It Measures When to Use It
Outlier Score (High to Low) Relative performance multiplier vs. median Default setting; ideal for finding breakout viral hooks that beat baseline distribution
Views (High to Low) Total raw reach and audience size Evaluating absolute scale and highest total view volume
Likes (High to Low) Direct positive feedback volume Identifying posts with high emotional resonance and affinity
Comments (High to Low) Discussion depth and interaction Spotting polarizing, debate-driving, or question-based formats
Newest First Chronological publication order Examining current content direction and recent creator iterations
Oldest First Earliest published items in sample Studying evergreen formats and earlier catalog performance

Handling of Hidden Metrics: When a creator hides public view counts or metrics are not yet available, Rank Kiwi normalizes the view count to null (displayed in the interface as —). To keep your analytical rankings clean and actionable, items with null views automatically sort to the very bottom of the grid rather than disrupting top performers.

Custom Filters

Open the filter panel to remove noise and isolate specific content formats.

Filter by Content Type

On supported creator surfaces, filter by dedicated video formats:

  • Reels (Instagram): Isolate vertical short-form video Reels. Rank Kiwi exclusively targets video Reels on Instagram directly from the creator's Reels tab.
  • Videos (YouTube): Focus exclusively on full long-form channel uploads with verified creator identity matching.
  • Shorts (YouTube): Filter to vertical short-form YouTube Shorts with live stats enrichment.

Filter by Outlier Multiplier

Set minimum performance thresholds to narrow large catalogs:

  • 2.0×+ — Shows videos that doubled typical creator performance.
  • 3.0×+ — Concentrates on content beating the creator's median by triple or more.
  • 5.0×+ — Restricts results to breakout viral anomalies beating the median by 5× to 10×.

Date Range Windows

Benchmark against relevant creator timeframes.

Creators evolve rapidly. A post that went viral two years ago might have operated under vastly different algorithmic rules or follower baselines than posts from this month.

Rank Kiwi's date filter allows you to restrict the active view to:

  • Last 30 Days: Instant view of current viral hooks and timely trends.
  • Last 90 Days: Standard quarter research window for consistent content planning.
  • All Time (Sample Scope): Explores historical breakout winners across the analyzed batch.

Recommended Research Filter Recipes

Proven filter combinations used by professional social researchers:

Recipe 1: The "Breakout Hook" Filter

Goal: Find short-form video hooks that dramatically exceeded typical creator distribution within the last 60 days.

  • Content Type: Reels Only
  • Outlier Score: ≥ 3.0×
  • Date Range: Last 60 Days
  • Sort Order: Outlier Score (High to Low)

Recipe 2: The "High-Engagement Discussion" Filter

Goal: Find topics that generated intense community discussion, debates, or questions.

  • Sort Order: Comments (High to Low)
  • Date Range: Last 90 Days
  • Action: Inspect the Content Details modal to examine the closing question or prompt.
