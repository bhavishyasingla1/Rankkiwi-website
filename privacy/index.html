Complete Privacy Policy: Chrome Extension & Website

All Scopes

Rank Kiwi clearly separates Chrome Extension execution (which is 100% local, sandboxed, and collects zero personal data) from our Marketing Website (rankkiwi.com, which uses standard privacy-respecting analytics and cookie consent). Use the buttons above to filter by environment.

100% Client-Side Sandbox: Calculations and Outlier Scores execute locally on your device.
Zero Remote Telemetry: No creator research history or viewed media is sent to Rank Kiwi servers.
Zero Account or Passwords: Works directly in your browser without login credentials or accounts.
Direct Local Exports: Excel (.xlsx), CSV, and JSON download straight to your local folder.

Extension & Website Overview

Rank Kiwi is a Google Chrome browser extension designed for researching creator content on Instagram and YouTube. This policy plainly explains what information is involved when you use the extension and when you visit our website.

Website Only Website Analytics, Microsoft Clarity & Google Analytics

When you visit the Rank Kiwi marketing website (rankkiwi.com), we utilize web analytics tools to understand visitor interactions and optimize site performance:

Microsoft Clarity & Advertising: We partner with Microsoft Clarity and Microsoft Advertising to capture how you use and interact with our website through behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session replay to improve and market our products/services. Website usage data is captured using first and third-party cookies and other tracking technologies to determine the popularity of products/services and online activity. Additionally, we use this information for site optimization, fraud/security purposes, and advertising. For more information about how Microsoft collects and uses your data, visit the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Google Analytics (gtag.js): We use Google Analytics (measurement ID: G-Y2V6BK8R00) to collect aggregated, anonymized telemetry about page views, referral sources, device types, and site navigation paths. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies to evaluate website usage without storing personal identification.

Cookie Consent & Control: You can accept or decline non-essential analytics tracking via our on-site cookie consent banner at any time, or configure your browser to block cookies and tracking scripts.

Chrome Extension Only Information processed

When you explicitly trigger an analysis, Rank Kiwi processes publicly available creator information displayed on the active webpage. This includes post titles, captions, publication dates, view counts, like counts, comment counts, and media URLs necessary to compute performance metrics and Outlier Scores.

Chrome Extension Only How it is used

This information is used strictly to display analysis, perform sorting, apply filters, and enable exporting or downloading at your request. Rank Kiwi does not use this information for advertising or user tracking.

Chrome Extension Only Local Processing & Direct Platform Communication

Local processing: Rank Kiwi calculates performance metrics, Outlier Scores, sorting, filtering, and export data locally in your browser.

Direct platform requests: When you use Rank Kiwi to analyze supported content, your browser communicates directly with Instagram or YouTube to retrieve the information required for analysis. Instagram requests may include the CSRF verification header described above.

No Rank Kiwi analytics backend: Rank Kiwi does not transmit analyzed creator content or research results to its own servers for storage or analytics. It does not sell this research data or share it with advertising networks or data brokers.

Local exports: Excel (.xlsx), CSV, and JSON exports are generated at your request and saved locally through your browser's download functionality.

Extension & Website Data sharing

Rank Kiwi does not sell, rent, or monetize your analyzed creator content or research activity. No analyzed data is transferred to third-party data brokers or marketing networks.

Chrome Extension Only Data retention

Because Rank Kiwi operates locally and does not operate a central content database, it does not retain a server-side history of your research. Any research exported as Excel (.xlsx), CSV, or JSON is saved directly to your local file system under your control.

Chrome Extension Only Authentication, Session & Platform Verification Handling

Rank Kiwi does not access or store your Instagram or YouTube passwords, login credentials, or private account session cookies.

When retrieving creator information from Instagram, Rank Kiwi reads Instagram's csrftoken value from the active page and includes it in the X-CSRFToken request header sent directly to Instagram. This token is used for request verification with Instagram and is not sent to Rank Kiwi's servers or unrelated third parties. Rank Kiwi does not intentionally persist this token in extension storage.

Rank Kiwi processes this token only for the relevant Instagram requests. Your use of Instagram and YouTube remains subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.

Chrome Extension Only Chrome Permissions & Manifest V3 Compliance

Rank Kiwi is engineered strictly following Chrome Extension Manifest V3 security requirements with the principle of least privilege. We declare and use only the following minimal permissions:

  • activeTab: Required exclusively when you explicitly click the "Analyze" button on an active Instagram creator profile or YouTube channel to evaluate publicly displayed posts.
  • storage: Used solely to preserve your local extension preferences (such as selected sample size 25/50/100, active sorting metric, and filter thresholds) within your local browser storage. No research data or personal information is synchronized to external clouds.
  • downloads: Used only when you explicitly trigger an export (Excel .xlsx, CSV, or JSON) or click "Download Reel" to save the file locally into your standard browser downloads folder.

Rank Kiwi does not request broad host permissions (<all_urls>), does not listen to web traffic in the background, does not inject external third-party tracking scripts into web pages, and does not read browsing history outside supported public creator pages.

Extension & Website Third-party platforms

Rank Kiwi functions on supported pages of Instagram and YouTube. Your use of those platforms is subject to their respective terms of service and privacy policies. Rank Kiwi is an independent extension and is not affiliated with Google, YouTube, Meta, or Instagram.

Chrome Extension Only Security

We build Rank Kiwi to adhere to modern browser extension security guidelines, using minimal browser permissions and avoiding external script injections.
