Privacy Policy

How Rank Kiwi processes information when you use the extension.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Overview

Rank Kiwi is a browser extension for researching creator content on Instagram and YouTube. This policy explains the information involved when you choose to use its features.

Information processed during analysis

When you request an analysis, Rank Kiwi processes the content and performance information available on the supported page, such as content titles or captions, URLs, views, likes, comments, and related creator-content information required for the feature you selected.

How information is used

This information is used to provide the requested analysis, sorting, filtering, copying, export, and download features. Rank Kiwi processes information needed to provide the analysis requested by the user. It is designed for creator research on supported Instagram and YouTube pages.

Client-side processing

Rank Kiwi performs requested analysis in your browser when you explicitly use the extension. Rank Kiwi does not require a separate Rank Kiwi account and does not maintain a server-side database of your analyzed content.

Data sharing

Rank Kiwi does not sell your analyzed content. We do not share it for advertising or data-broker purposes.

Data retention

Because Rank Kiwi does not maintain a server-side database of your analyzed content, it does not retain a server-side history of the content you analyze. Information you copy, download, or export is handled by you and the applications or storage locations you choose.

Authentication and browser sessions

Rank Kiwi does not create a separate Rank Kiwi account. If you are signed in to Instagram or YouTube in your browser, those services handle your session under their own terms and privacy policies.

Third-party platforms

Rank Kiwi operates on supported third-party websites. Their collection and processing of information are governed by their own policies. Rank Kiwi is an independent third-party extension and is not affiliated with Google, YouTube, Meta, or Instagram.

Security

We aim to keep the extension and its distribution secure. As with any browser extension or website, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Rank Kiwi changes. The current version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact us through the Rank Kiwi support page.
