Privacy Policy

How Rank Kiwi processes information when you use the extension.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Overview

Rank Kiwi is a Google Chrome browser extension designed for researching creator content on Instagram and YouTube. This policy plainly explains what information is involved when you use the extension.

Information processed

When you explicitly trigger an analysis, Rank Kiwi processes publicly available creator information displayed on the active webpage. This includes post titles, captions, publication dates, view counts, like counts, comment counts, and media URLs necessary to compute performance metrics and Outlier Scores.

How it is used

This information is used strictly to display analysis, perform sorting, apply filters, and enable exporting or downloading at your request. Rank Kiwi does not use this information for advertising or user tracking.

Browser-based processing

All analysis runs directly in your local browser sandbox. Rank Kiwi does not require a separate Rank Kiwi user account and does not maintain a server-side database of your analyzed content.

Data sharing

Rank Kiwi does not sell, rent, or monetize your analyzed creator content or research activity. No analyzed data is transferred to third-party data brokers or marketing networks.

Data retention

Because Rank Kiwi operates locally and does not operate a central content database, it does not retain a server-side history of your research. Any research exported as CSV or JSON is saved directly to your local file system under your control.

Authentication/session handling

Rank Kiwi operates independently of your private credentials. It never accesses, intercepts, or transmits your Instagram or YouTube passwords, login tokens, or authentication cookies.

Third-party platforms

Rank Kiwi functions on supported pages of Instagram and YouTube. Your use of those platforms is subject to their respective terms of service and privacy policies. Rank Kiwi is an independent extension and is not affiliated with Google, YouTube, Meta, or Instagram.

Security

We build Rank Kiwi to adhere to modern browser extension security guidelines, using minimal browser permissions and avoiding external script injections.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or Rank Kiwi data practices, please email us directly at support@hibrushed.com or visit our Support page.
