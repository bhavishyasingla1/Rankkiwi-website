The $15/Month Problem: Why Analytics Tools Are Broken

If you have ever tried to research competitor hooks on Instagram or YouTube, you know the frustration. The software landscape is split into two extremes:

  • Bloated Enterprise Suites: Platforms like Metricool, Sprout Social, and Socialinsider charge anywhere from $18 to $99 per month. They are designed for enterprise reporting teams, require connecting full business accounts, and take five minutes just to navigate to a content list.
  • Gated Single-Purpose Extensions: Small extensions that promise to sort feeds, but cut off your results after 10 videos, inject tracking pixels into your browser, or aggressively prompt you to upgrade to a Pro tier.

For solo creators, video editors pitching clients, and agile agency strategists, neither of these makes sense. What you actually need is answers in two seconds:

  1. What is this creator's true typical baseline? (Median views, not average)
  2. Which specific Reels or videos beat that baseline by 3× to 10×?
  3. Can I save the reference video or export the rows to a spreadsheet without paying?

Rank Kiwi answers all three directly on the page, with zero lag.

Why Median Math Matters (Averages Lie)

Almost every generic analytics tool uses mathematical averages (the arithmetic mean). On social platforms, averages are mathematically broken.

Consider a creator who uploads 20 Reels. Nineteen of them get between 10,000 and 15,000 views. One video goes massively viral and hits 2,000,000 views.

The Mean Average: Calculates to ~110,000 views. Now, a solid Reel with 50,000 views looks like a failure because of one statistical anomaly.

The Median Baseline: Remains exactly 12,500 views. Now, you can instantly see that the 50,000-view Reel is a genuine 4.0× Outlier, while the 2,000,000 hit is a breakout phenomenon.

Rank Kiwi calculates the true mathematical median from visible uploads so your baseline is never corrupted by a single fluke.

Our Four Engineering Principles

1. 100% Free Forever

No paid tiers, no 7-day trials, and no "upgrade to unlock full exports." Rank Kiwi will never ask you for a credit card.

2. Zero Tracking & No Passwords

All processing runs 100% locally in your Chrome sandbox. We never ask for your Instagram or Google passwords and collect zero personal telemetry.

3. Accuracy Over Hype

We don't claim impossible metrics like "top 1% of the world." We benchmark every video against the creator's own proven median. And on Instagram, we strictly analyze video Reels because photo views aren't publicly exposed.

4. Fast, Native, Client-Side

No heavy remote scrapers that get rate-limited. Rank Kiwi reads the clean DOM nodes already rendered in your browser and displays multipliers in under 200 milliseconds.
