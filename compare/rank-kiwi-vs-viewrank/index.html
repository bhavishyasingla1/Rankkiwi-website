Rank Kiwi vs ViewRank: Free Instagram Reels Research Extensions Compared

Both tools are 100% free Chrome extensions designed for short-form video research on Instagram. But they take fundamentally different approaches to scoring, organization, and export workflows. Here is a transparent breakdown of where each tool wins.

By Bhavishya Singla • Last Updated: October 2026 • Extension Version: v1.0.1

Quick Verdict: Which should you install?

Choose ViewRank if: You only research Instagram Reels, want to tag videos with custom notes, bookmark favorite hooks into saved lists, and enjoy watching Reels inside an embedded dashboard panel.

Choose Rank Kiwi if: You research both Instagram Reels and YouTube (Shorts & videos), want mathematical Outlier Scores based on creator medians (rather than raw view sorts), need one-click MP4 video downloads from creator profiles and the Explore feed, and export to Excel (.xlsx) or Google Sheets.

Where ViewRank Wins (And Why We Admire It)

Honest comparisons must give credit where competitors excel. ViewRank built several thoughtful features specifically tailored to video editors who live exclusively inside Instagram:

1. Notes and Tagging Directly on Reels

ViewRank lets you attach quick tags (e.g. "Hook idea", "Sound design", "Client pitch") and notes right on scanned posts. This keeps your qualitative notes adjacent to the post rather than having to jump into a separate Notion doc.

2. Built-in Favorites System

Instead of exporting a CSV every time you find an interesting video, ViewRank lets you click a star icon to save Reels into an in-extension collection.

3. Granular Scan Depth (50 to 500 Reels)

ViewRank provides a slider allowing creators to scan deep catalogs up to 500 Reels. Rank Kiwi currently bounds sample sizes to 25, 50, and 100 to protect Chrome browser memory and keep scan speeds instant.

4. Persistent Filter Memory

When you set a minimum view count or sorting order in ViewRank, it remembers your preference across creator pages. Rank Kiwi resets to default median sorting on each new profile visit to ensure fresh baseline calculation.

Where Rank Kiwi Wins

Rank Kiwi was engineered specifically around mathematical outlier benchmarking, multi-platform creator audits, and flexible spreadsheet export workflows:

1. Dual-Platform: Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts & Videos

Short-form video research rarely stops at Instagram. Most creators publish across both Reels and YouTube Shorts. Rank Kiwi allows you to inspect YouTube channels, benchmark long-form uploads, filter Shorts, and run anti-leakage validation without installing a second tool.

2. Mathematical Outlier Score (Median Baseline)

Sorting by raw views tells you which video got the most views, but it doesn't tell you if a video broke out relative to that creator's typical performance. Rank Kiwi computes each creator's median views and scores every video (e.g. 6.2×). A 300K Reel on a 50K-median account is an explosive outlier; on a 1M-median account, it's an underperformer.

3. Instagram Explore Feed Video Downloads

Rank Kiwi adds direct one-click MP4 downloading to Instagram's Explore feed (instagram.com/explore/). When you find a trending hook or transition in Explore, you can download the video instantly for offline editing breakdowns.

4. Native Excel (.xlsx) and Rich Clipboard Copying

While ViewRank outputs simple CSV files, Rank Kiwi generates formatted Excel (.xlsx) files and provides a one-click Copy to Clipboard feature that pastes formatted rows directly into Google Sheets, Notion tables, or Airtable.
