Rank Kiwi vs Sort Feed: Which Creator Research Tool Is Better?

Which tool is better for creator content research and outlier discovery? We compare features, workflow, platform coverage, depth, transcription, privacy, and pricing based on official first-party documentation.

By Bhavishya Singla • Last reviewed: October 5, 2026 • Sourced from official docs & pricing
Editorial Transparency: This comparison is published by Rank Kiwi. We aim to describe competing products objectively, state their verified capabilities, and link directly to primary documentation so you can evaluate trade-offs for yourself.
Quick Answer

The Short Summary

Rank Kiwi is built specifically for fast, lightweight, client-side creator research across Instagram and YouTube. It provides full Outlier Score analysis, sorting, filtering, and data exports directly in the browser completely free, with zero paywalls on visible feed analysis.

Sort Feed is a multi-platform creator research extension supporting Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and YouTube. Its free tier covers the latest 25 posts or one week of content, while its paid Pro plan ($10/month billed annually or $15/month monthly) unlocks scanning up to 1,000 posts, custom date ranges, and 500 minutes per month of cloud-powered audio transcription.

Choose Rank Kiwi if your primary focus is Instagram and YouTube and you want a free, private, browser-native tool without post-count limits. Choose Sort Feed if your workflow relies on researching TikTok or Facebook, or if you need automated speech-to-text audio transcription.

Core Positioning: Best For

Rank Kiwi is Best For

Creators, editors, and strategists seeking 100% free, unlimited outlier analysis focused specifically on Instagram Reels & posts and YouTube videos & Shorts, with complete client-side data privacy.

Sort Feed is Best For

Content teams and agencies that require TikTok and Facebook coverage in addition to Instagram/YouTube, need large historical batches (up to 1,000 posts), or rely on audio transcription.

What Rank Kiwi Does

Rank Kiwi is a browser extension designed for content creators, video editors, and social strategists who need to rapidly analyze performance on Instagram and YouTube without switching to an external dashboard or paying recurring software subscriptions.

When you visit any public creator profile or video grid on Instagram or YouTube, Rank Kiwi inspects the rendered content, extracts engagement metrics (views, likes, comments), and calculates an Outlier Score for every piece of content. The Outlier Score uses a creator-relative statistical baseline (evaluating performance against the creator's own median rather than arbitrary follower counts), making it simple to spot viral breakout hooks immediately.

All processing is performed locally in the user's browser. There are no proprietary databases, no tracking logins, and no paywalls limiting how many posts you can inspect on a creator's page. Users can export all analyzed data to CSV or JSON formats for further research in spreadsheets or Notion.

What Sort Feed Does

Sort Feed is a browser extension created to help users sort and discover high-performing content across four major social platforms: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and YouTube.

Sort Feed injects sorting controls into supported social media feeds, allowing users to reorder posts by view counts, engagement, and its own implementation of an Outlier Score. According to Sort Feed's official documentation, its Outlier Score compares a given post against the creator's median benchmark over recent posts to highlight content that outperformed expectations.

Sort Feed operates on a freemium business model. On its Free plan, users can sort and view the latest 25 posts or one week of content. For users who need deeper historical scanning (up to 1,000 posts), custom post counts, or video dialogue transcription (500 minutes per month), Sort Feed offers a paid Pro subscription priced at $10 per month (billed yearly at $120) or $15 per month billed month-to-month.

Workflow Comparison

Rank Kiwi Workflow

  1. Install the free extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Navigate directly to any creator's Instagram profile or YouTube channel in your browser.
  3. Rank Kiwi extracts metrics and applies Outlier Scores to the visible feed instantly.
  4. Sort by Outlier Score or engagement, filter by keywords, and export data directly to CSV.

Sort Feed Workflow

  1. Install the Sort Feed extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Navigate to Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, or YouTube.
  3. Activate Sort Feed's overlay controls to sort the latest 25 posts.
  4. To scan past 25 posts or transcribe spoken audio, log into a paid Pro account.

Where Each Tool is Stronger

Where Sort Feed is Stronger

  • Platform Versatility: Sort Feed covers TikTok and Facebook in addition to Instagram and YouTube. If you actively research short-form content on TikTok, Sort Feed has a distinct advantage.
  • Audio Transcription: Sort Feed Pro includes 500 minutes per month of automated transcription, making it useful for creators who want to analyze spoken video scripts.
  • Extended Deep Scans: On Pro, users can batch-scan up to 1,000 historical posts with customized date range limits.
  • Media Downloader: Dedicated one-click downloading for images and videos directly from supported platforms.

Where Rank Kiwi is Stronger

  • No Post-Count Paywall: Rank Kiwi does not artificially cap research at 25 posts or 1 week. You can analyze full loaded feeds on Instagram and YouTube without paying a monthly subscription.
  • Zero Cost: Rank Kiwi provides Outlier Scores, sorting, filtering, and data exports completely free without requiring credit cards or trials.
  • Strict Client-Side Privacy: All metric extraction and statistical computations occur locally in your browser. No video audio is sent to third-party transcription APIs.
  • Focused Specialization: Dedicated DOM collectors built specifically for Instagram's and YouTube's evolving web interfaces ensure high extraction reliability.

Product Limitations

Sort Feed Limitations

  • The free plan restricts analysis to the latest 25 posts or 7 days, which is insufficient for auditing older channel back-catalogs.
  • Pro plan requires a paid subscription ($120/year or $15/month).
  • Cloud transcription requires transmitting video audio to external servers.

Rank Kiwi Limitations

  • Does not support TikTok or Facebook; coverage is strictly Instagram and YouTube.
  • Does not currently feature automated speech-to-text audio transcription.
  • Analysis is tied to rendered on-page content rather than a centralized historical database.

The Editorial Verdict

Rank Kiwi and Sort Feed are both effective extensions that solve a genuine creator problem: uncovering high-performing content without manually scrolling through hundreds of videos. Neither tool is universally "better" for everyone; the right choice comes down to platform requirements and budget.

If your research revolves around Instagram and YouTube, Rank Kiwi offers a superior free experience because it provides full outlier scoring, sorting, and exports with no 25-post paywall. However, if your business requires TikTok, Facebook, or audio transcription, Sort Feed Pro is a well-built tool that justifies its subscription cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rank Kiwi really free, or is there a hidden paywall?
Rank Kiwi is completely free to install and use. All features—including the Outlier Score, sorting, metric filters, and CSV/JSON exports—are fully accessible without payment or artificial scan limits.
What are the free limits on Sort Feed?
According to Sort Feed's official documentation as of October 5, 2026, the free plan allows users to analyze the latest 25 posts or one week of content on supported platforms. Scanning beyond 25 posts requires a paid Pro plan.
How do their Outlier Score calculations differ?
Both tools use creator-relative benchmarking comparing posts against the creator's median performance rather than relying on misleading raw view counts. Rank Kiwi evaluates loaded feed content dynamically in real-time, while Sort Feed evaluates against recent historical posts defined by its collection window.
Does Rank Kiwi support TikTok or Facebook?
No. Rank Kiwi is intentionally focused on Instagram and YouTube to provide the cleanest, highest-reliability DOM parsing for those two platforms. If you require TikTok or Facebook research, Sort Feed is the better choice.
Does either tool require my social media password?
No. Neither Rank Kiwi nor Sort Feed ever asks for your Instagram, YouTube, or Google passwords. Both operate locally on top of public web pages you visit in your browser.
How does Sort Feed's audio transcription work?
Sort Feed Pro includes 500 minutes of transcription per month. When activated, the video audio is processed through a cloud-based speech-to-text service to generate written scripts. Rank Kiwi does not offer transcription.
Can I export data to CSV with both tools?
Yes. Both Rank Kiwi and Sort Feed support exporting extracted post data and metrics to CSV and JSON formats for spreadsheet analysis.
