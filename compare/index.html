CATEGORY TAXONOMY

Understanding the Tool Landscape

Not every analytics tool serves the same purpose. We distinguish three core categories:

Category 1

Creator Content Research

Browser-based tools designed to reverse-engineer competitor profiles, calculate Outlier Scores, sort visible posts, and identify viral hooks directly on-page.

Examples: Rank Kiwi, Sort Feed, Viewstats
Category 2

Social Analytics & Management

Cloud dashboards built for cross-network scheduling, automated client reporting, multi-brand monitoring, and unified social inboxes.

Examples: Socialinsider, Metricool
Category 3

Native Platform Analytics

First-party platform dashboards providing deep ground-truth analytics (revenue, retention drop-off graphs, subscriber demographics) for owned channels.

Examples: YouTube Studio, Meta Business Suite
