FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Rank Kiwi?+

Rank Kiwi is a Chrome extension for researching creator content on Instagram and YouTube. It lets you sort content by performance and find the posts worth studying.

How does Rank Kiwi work?+

Open an Instagram profile or YouTube channel in your browser and run an analysis. Rank Kiwi inspects visible post metrics directly on your screen and calculates Outlier Scores.

Is Rank Kiwi free?+

Yes. Rank Kiwi is completely free to install and use. There are no subscriptions, tiers, or paywalls.

Do I need an account?+

No. Rank Kiwi does not require creating an account or logging into any service.

Which Instagram content can I analyze?+

You can analyze Instagram Reels, standard posts, and multi-slide carousels on supported creator profile pages.

Which YouTube content can I analyze?+

You can analyze long-form YouTube videos and YouTube Shorts on supported channel pages.

What is Outlier Score?+

Outlier Score compares an individual post's metrics to the creator's median baseline. A 3.2× score means the post reached roughly triple the creator's typical result.

Does Rank Kiwi store my analyzed content?+

No. Analysis runs locally in your browser. Rank Kiwi does not maintain a server-side database of your analyzed creator content.

Can I export my research?+

Yes. You can copy the data directly to your clipboard or export it as clean CSV or JSON files.
