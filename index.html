FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about Rank Kiwi and how it works.

What does Rank Kiwi actually do?+

Rank Kiwi is a creator research tool for Instagram and YouTube. Open any creator in your niche or YouTube channel, and Rank Kiwi inspects visible content to return the grid sorted by Outlier Score. From there, you can inspect metrics, read captions, and export everything into a spreadsheet to build your content plan.

How is that different from scrolling the profile myself?+

Manual scrolling only shows posts in chronologic order with no relative performance context. Rank Kiwi automatically calculates Outlier Scores comparing each post against the creator’s median baseline, pinpointing the top 1% anomaly posts that drastically outperformed the rest.

What is an Outlier Score?+

Outlier Score measures how many times better a post performed relative to the creator's median benchmark. A 3.2× score means the post earned more than triple the creator's typical views or engagement, highlighting an exceptional hook or format.

Which platforms and content formats are supported?+

Rank Kiwi supports Instagram Reels, standard grid posts, and carousels, as well as YouTube long-form videos and YouTube Shorts.

Do you need my password? Does anything leave my computer?+

No. Rank Kiwi never accesses, requires, or stores your passwords or login credentials. All analysis runs 100% locally in your browser sandbox, and your analyzed content is not sent to any central server database.

Can I export the data to Google Sheets or Excel?+

Yes. You can copy the data directly to your clipboard or download structured CSV and JSON files ready to import into Google Sheets, Notion, or Excel.

Is Rank Kiwi really 100% free?+

Yes. Rank Kiwi is completely free to install and use from the Chrome Web Store. There are no subscriptions or paywalls.
