FAQ

Questions answered.

Quick answers about Rank Kiwi.

What does Rank Kiwi do?+

Rank Kiwi is a research extension for Instagram and YouTube. Open any creator or channel to rank visible posts by Outlier Score and export directly to a spreadsheet.

How is this different from scrolling?+

Scrolling only shows posts chronologically. Rank Kiwi calculates Outlier Scores against the creator's median benchmark to reveal viral anomaly posts.

What is an Outlier Score?+

Outlier Score measures performance relative to typical results. A 3.2× score means the post earned more than triple the creator's median views or engagement.

Which platforms are supported?+

Rank Kiwi supports Instagram Reels, posts, and carousels, plus YouTube long-form videos and Shorts.

Do you need my password?+

No. Rank Kiwi never accesses or requires passwords. Analysis runs 100% locally in your browser sandbox.

Can I export to Google Sheets?+

Yes. You can copy results to your clipboard or download clean CSV and JSON files.

Is Rank Kiwi free?+

Yes. Rank Kiwi is completely free to install and use from the Chrome Web Store.
