FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Quick answers about privacy, browser support, and Outlier Scores.

What does Rank Kiwi do?+

Rank Kiwi is a free Chrome research extension for Instagram and YouTube. Open any creator or channel to rank visible Reels, Shorts, and videos against their own median baseline and export directly to a spreadsheet.

How does the Outlier Score work?+

Outlier Score divides a video's view count by the creator's median view count. A 3.2× score means the video earned more than triple the creator's typical results. We use medians instead of averages so one viral hit doesn't distort the baseline.

Why Instagram Reels only?+

Instagram does not expose view counts on static photos or carousels in public grid views. To protect your data accuracy and keep baselines pure, Rank Kiwi focuses exclusively on video Reels and Explore feed video downloads.

How does the Explore download feature work?+

When browsing Instagram's Explore feed or creator Reels grids, Rank Kiwi adds a direct download action so you can save high-performing reference videos straight to your disk for editing breakdowns.

Which YouTube formats are supported?+

Rank Kiwi supports YouTube long-form videos and YouTube Shorts, allowing you to filter by duration and benchmark against channel medians.

Do you require my Instagram or Google password?+

Never. Rank Kiwi requires no accounts, no login, and no passwords. All data processing executes 100% locally in your Chrome browser sandbox.

Can I export to Google Sheets?+

Yes. You can export directly to native Excel (.xlsx), standard CSV, or copy formatted rows to your clipboard for instant pasting into Google Sheets, Notion, or Airtable.

Is Rank Kiwi really free forever?+

Yes. There are no paid tiers, no subscription prompts, and no locked features. It is free forever.
