The 3 Tiers of Social Media Competitor Analysis

To avoid paying for features you will never use, match your problem to the appropriate software tier:

Tier 1: Enterprise Social Listening

Monitors untagged brand mentions, customer sentiment, share of voice, and crisis PR across millions of public web and social discussions. Requires heavy enterprise tools like Sprout Social or Brandwatch ($200–$1,000+/mo).

Tier 2: Multi-Brand Benchmarking

Tracks rival brand profiles side-by-side to compare overall follower growth velocity, posting cadence, and average engagement rates for executive reporting. Handled by platforms like Socialinsider ($82+/mo) and Metricool ($20/mo).

Tier 3: Content-Level Outlier Research

Inspects individual competitor videos, Reels, and hooks to find breakout outlier content that beat typical channel medians. Handled by focused tools like Rank Kiwi (free).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between competitive benchmarking and social listening? +

Competitive benchmarking compares public profile metrics (follower growth, posting frequency, average engagement rates) across known rival accounts. Social listening scans millions of public online discussions to detect untagged brand mentions, sentiment shifts, and consumer conversations even when the competitor is not explicitly tagged.

Can competitor analysis tools see private profile metrics? +

No. All third-party competitor analysis tools rely exclusively on publicly visible information (views, likes, comments, upload dates). They cannot access competitor retention drop-off graphs, private audience demographics, reach counts, or advertising spend.

How can I analyze competitor content without paying $80+/month? +

If your primary goal is content research—finding which videos and Reels outperformed a competitor's normal baseline—Rank Kiwi provides 100% free, unrestricted in-browser feed sorting and Outlier Score calculation with zero subscription fees.
